Privacy Policy

Aquarium · Last updated: 2026-11-26

Other languages ↓

In short

Aquarium collects no data. Everything you log in the app stays on your iPhone or iPad.

  • No account, no ads, no tracking: the app contains no analytics or advertising tools, and does not identify you in any way.

Your tanks, readings and fish

Your tanks, your water readings, your maintenance tasks and your list of fish are saved in the app's private storage, on your device. They are not sent to anyone, not even the developer.

Deleting the app erases all of this data. It may be included in your device's iCloud or local backups, which you manage yourself in Apple's settings.

Notifications

Maintenance reminders are scheduled on the device; no server is involved.

Links to help

The “Help and Contact” and “Privacy” links open this page and the help page in your browser. The app sends no information to them.

Purchases

Aquarium Pro is sold and managed by Apple. The app sees neither your payment method nor your identity; it only reads, through the App Store, whether the purchase was made, in order to unlock the features.

Children

Aquarium collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
