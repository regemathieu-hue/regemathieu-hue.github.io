Privacy Policy

Wi-Fi Map · Last updated: 2026-11-02

Other languages ↓

In short

Wi-Fi Map doesn't collect any personal data, and the developer has no server. Your scans are saved on your device. The only internet connection is used to measure your Wi-Fi, by downloading data from a test server.

Your scans

Each scan contains its name, its date, the Wi-Fi network name when available, the speed and latency measurements, and the path you walked. This path is expressed in metres relative to your starting point: it isn't a geographic location.

Scans and settings are saved on your device, and the developer has no access to them. Like other apps' data, they may be included in your device backups if you've turned them on. You can delete a scan at any time; deleting the app erases them all.

Wi-Fi measurement and test server

During a scan, the app downloads small blocks of data from a test server: by default speed.cloudflare.com, operated by Cloudflare. As with any internet connection, this server sees your IP address and the download request, which states the block size and a random number that prevents caching.

Neither your network name, your scans nor your results are sent to it. Cloudflare handles these connections under its own privacy policy. If you choose another server in the settings, that server receives these connections instead.

Permissions requested

You can withdraw each permission in your device's Settings.

  • Camera: to follow your position in your home during a scan. No image is saved or sent. “By finger” mode lets you do without it.
  • Location: iOS requires it to read the Wi-Fi network name. The app doesn't read your GPS coordinates. The network name stays on your device.

Sharing

You can share a scan as an image with iOS tools (AirDrop, Messages, Mail…). The image shows the scan's name, date, map and statistics, but not the network name. Nothing is shared unless you ask.

Ads, purchases and tracking

Wi-Fi Map is free and requires no account. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no trackers and no analytics or usage statistics tools.

Children

The app doesn't collect any data and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
