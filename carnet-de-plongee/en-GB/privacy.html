Privacy Policy

Dive Log · Last updated: 2026-11-26

Other languages ↓

In short

Dive Log collects no data. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone or iPad.

  • No account, no ads, no tracking: the app contains no analytics or advertising tools, and does not identify you in any way.
  • No server: the app only connects to the internet for the purchase of Pro, which is handled by Apple.

Health data

With your permission, the app reads two measurements recorded by your Apple Watch in the Health app: underwater depth and water temperature. It uses them only to find your dives and draw their profile.

  • These measurements are processed on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • They are not used for advertising or profiling, and are not shared with anyone.
  • The app writes nothing to Health and reads no other health data.
  • You can withdraw the permission at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

Your log

Your dives, notes and certifications are stored in the app's private storage, on your device. Deleting them (removing a dive, or deleting the app) erases them. They may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which you manage yourself in Apple's settings.

Card photos

If you add a photo of a certification card, the app only receives the photo you choose yourself; it has no access to the rest of your photo library. That photo is kept on your device, with the log.

Exports

PDF, CSV and UDDF files are created on your device. You choose where to send or save them; the app does not share them with anyone.

Purchases

The purchase of Pro is sold and handled by Apple. The app sees neither your payment method nor your identity; it only reads, through the App Store, whether the purchase was made, in order to unlock the features.

Children

Dive Log collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
