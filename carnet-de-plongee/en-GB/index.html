FAQ

How do I import the dives from my Apple Watch Ultra?

There is nothing to install on the watch. Underwater, Apple Watch Ultra records the depth (down to 40 metres) and the water temperature by itself in the Health app on your iPhone. In Dive Log, open the Log tab, tap "+" then "Import from Apple Watch", and finally "Find my dives". The first time, iOS asks for permission to read these two measurements. The app then finds each dive with its date, duration, depth and profile; you only have to add the site, your buddy or the tank pressure.

The import finds no dives. What should I do?

First check that the dive was made with an Apple Watch Ultra and that the watch has had time to sync with the iPhone (keep them close for a few minutes, with the Health app open). Then check the permission: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices, choose the app, and turn on "Underwater Depth" and "Water Temperature". Also try a longer period ("All history"). Immersions shorter than 2 minutes or shallower than 1.5 metres are left out.

Can I keep my log without an Apple Watch?

Yes. Tap "+" then "Enter a dive" and write down whatever you want: date, duration, depth, site, buddy, temperatures, visibility, gas, tank pressures, weight, suit, dive type, rating and notes. Nothing is required. If you later import the same dive from the watch, the app completes your entry with the profile instead of creating a duplicate.

I had already dived before using this log. How do I keep my numbering?

In Settings, enter the number of "Dives before this log". Numbering and totals take it into account: with 120 dives already made, the first one in the log is number 121. Dives are always numbered by date, even if you add an old one afterwards.

What does Pro include and how much does it cost?

The log, manual entry, the dive profile and the import from Apple Watch are free and unlimited. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription; its price is shown in the app before you buy. It adds statistics (records, dives by year and by season, depths, favourite sites and buddies, air consumption), exports (a printable PDF logbook with a box for the dive centre's stamp, a CSV spreadsheet, the UDDF format used by dive software) and certifications (levels, specialities, medical certificate, with a photo of each card and a warning before it expires).

How do I get my purchase back on a new device?

Open Settings › "Discover Pro" and tap "Restore my purchase", with the App Store account you used to buy it. You do not pay a second time.

Can I change units?

Yes: in Settings › Units, choose metres, °C and bar, or feet, °F and psi. Your dives are not changed: only the display changes, including in exports.

Where are my dives stored? How do I back them up?

Only on your device, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no server, and the log is not synced between your devices. It is part of your iPhone's iCloud backup or computer backup, which you manage in Apple's settings. With Pro, you can also keep a copy by exporting the log (Settings › "Export the log"). Deleting the app erases the log from the device.

Can I use the app to plan a dive or to dive safely?

No. Dive Log keeps a record of your dives afterwards. It is neither a dive computer nor a planning tool: never use it to decide on a depth, a duration or a decompression stop. Always dive with the proper equipment and within the limits of your training.
