Privacy Policy

Atelier Fichiers · Last updated: 2026-10-22

Other languages ↓

In short

Atelier Fichiers does not collect, store on a server or transmit any personal data. All file processing takes place on your device. The developer has no access to any of your files or to any information about you.

Your files

The files you open in the app (documents, PDFs, images, audio, videos, archives…) are processed locally and saved wherever you choose. The app never sends them over the internet.

The history of operations is kept only on your device. You can clear it at any time from the app.

Permissions requested

Each permission is only requested when you use the related feature, and you can revoke it in your device's Settings.

  • Camera: to scan documents and read codes. Images stay on the device.
  • Photos (add only): to save converted images to your photo library. The app does not read your library; you pick the photos to import yourself.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe your recordings into text and create subtitles. Transcription is done on the device, without sending anything.
  • Notifications: to let you know when a long operation has finished. These notifications are generated locally.

Smart features and translation

Summaries, recipe extraction and business card reading use the Apple Intelligence model built into the device. Translation uses Apple's on-device translation service: only the language downloads go through Apple. In both cases, your text is never transmitted to the developer.

In-app purchases

The Atelier Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app only checks whether the purchase was made; it receives no payment data. The free operations counter is stored on your device.

Advertising and tracking

Atelier Fichiers contains no advertising, no trackers and no analytics or usage statistics tools.

Children

The app collects no data and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
