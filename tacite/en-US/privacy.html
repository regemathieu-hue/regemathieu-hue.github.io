Privacy Policy

Tacite · Last updated: 2026-11-12

Other languages ↓

In summary

Tacite does not collect any personal data. The app works offline, never connects to any server, requires no account, and has no access to your bank. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone, unless you choose to export it.

Your data

The app stores the subscriptions you enter (name, price, currency, frequency, dates, category, color, payment method, and notes) and your settings. The 'payment method' field is free text: never enter a card number here.

These data are stored on your iPhone, in an area shared only with the Tacite widget, and the developer does not have access to them. Like other app data, they may appear in your iPhone backups if you have enabled them. Deleting the app erases them.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications: they are scheduled and displayed by your iPhone itself, without going through any server. They contain the subscription name, amount, and payment date. You can disable them at any time in iOS Settings.

Export

Nothing leaves the app without your action. The CSV export contains your subscriptions: you choose where to send or save it, and it is then subject to the terms of the chosen service.

Permissions requested

Tacite only requests permission to display notifications, for reminders. No camera, location, contacts, or photos.

Advertising, purchases, and tracking

Tacite is free. It contains no ads, in-app purchases, trackers, or tools for analytics or usage statistics.

Children

The app does not collect any data and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any questions about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
