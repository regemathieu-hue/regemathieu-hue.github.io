FAQ

Are exchange rates updated in real time?

Exchange rates are refreshed each time the app opens if an internet connection is available. An internet connection is required to get the latest rates.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes, every conversion except currencies works offline. Currency conversions use the last cached rate.

How do I change the app's language?

The language can be changed directly in the app's settings. More than 16 languages are available.
