FAQ

How do I earn credits faster?

Follow the goal shown at the top of the shop: each one pays a reward. Complete research projects as soon as they appear, and keep tapping the processor to fill the overclock gauge: your output is tripled for a few seconds. Also catch the golden packets that drift across the screen.

Why is my output slowing down?

Your servers are short on power: the gauge under your credits turns red and the edges of the screen flash. Open the Energy tab and buy a power source; the “Best price per watt” label shows the most cost-effective one.

What should I do when an incident appears?

A rack is overheating: tap it several times. A DDoS attack: tap the malicious requests before they reach the processor. A client in a hurry: accept their contract and earn the requested amount in time. A handled incident pays credits; a missed one slows your output for 30 seconds.

What does going public do?

It resets your game in exchange for shares. Each share boosts all your output forever, and you can spend them on the board of directors for permanent upgrades, such as starting capital or auto-buy. The right time: when the Market tab says your output would double.

Do my servers keep working when the app is closed?

Yes, at half speed at first and for up to two hours. Some research projects and the board of directors extend this time and raise the rate. In the game settings (gear icon), you can ask for a reminder when storage is full.

Is my game saved?

Yes, automatically, on your device. It is not synced between devices and is lost if you delete the app.

How do I start over?

Tap the gear icon at the top left, then “Start over”. Careful: your game, shares and achievements are permanently erased.
