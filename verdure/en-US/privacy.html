Privacy Policy

Verdure · Last updated: 2026-11-21

Other languages ↓

In short

Verdure collects no data. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone or iPad.

  • No account, no ads, no tracking: Verdure includes no analytics or advertising tools and does not identify you in any way.

Plant recognition on your device

Photos you take or choose are analyzed by a model installed in the app, without any connection. They are not sent anywhere.

Camera and photo library

The camera is only used to frame a plant inside the app. The photo library is only read for photos you pick yourself (recognition, photo journal). Nothing is read in the background.

Your plants, journal and photos

They are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Removing a plant or deleting the app erases them. They may be included in your device's iCloud or iTunes backups, which you manage yourself in Apple's settings.

Notifications

Watering and care reminders are scheduled on the device; no server is involved.

Purchases

The Verdure+ subscription is sold and managed by Apple. Verdure sees neither your payment method nor your identity; the app only checks through the App Store whether a subscription is active, to unlock features.

Reference photos

The photos on care sheets come from iNaturalist and Wikimedia Commons (free licenses) and are bundled in the app; credits are in Settings › Photo credits.

Children

Verdure collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
