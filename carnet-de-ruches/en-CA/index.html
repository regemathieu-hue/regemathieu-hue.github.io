FAQ

Where do I start?

In the Apiaries tab, create your apiary, then tap “Add a Hive”: the app suggests a name, and you can set the hive type and the queen's birth year. Then open Settings › Holding to enter your name, your address and your beekeeper number (NAPI in France): they make up the header of the register. Under its icon, the app is called “My Hives”.

How do I record an inspection quickly?

Open the hive and tap “Inspection”, or swipe the hive's row to the right in the list. Nothing is required: tap only what you saw (queen, frames of bees and brood, stores, queen cells, signs observed, varroa count, supers, feeding). Tapping a choice again clears it.

How do I record a varroa treatment?

Tap “Treatment” from a hive, or “Treat the Whole Apiary” in the apiary's ⋯ menu. “Choose an Authorised Medicine” fills in the name, the active substance, the withdrawal period and the planned end date. The list contains the medicines authorised in France, and everything can be edited: the medicine's leaflet prevails. Add the quantity per hive and the supplier, which the register asks for. On the planned end date the app sends you a reminder, and “Removed Today” closes the treatment.

Is the app's register enough in case of an official inspection?

It gathers the sections that France requires for bees under the order of 5 June 2000: holding, health supervision, apiaries, colony movements and treatments, plus harvests. The register must be available on paper: export it as a PDF, print it regularly and keep it for five years, with the prescriptions and the invoices or delivery notes for medicines. The app does not replace the official texts or the advice of your veterinarian or your beekeepers' health association; if in doubt, ask your local animal health authority. Outside France, check the rules of your own country.

How do I move hives from one apiary to another?

Open the hive, then ⋯ › Move; for several hives at once, use the apiary's ⋯ menu › Move the Hives. Choose the destination apiary and the date: the move is recorded in the register's movements. Having several apiaries requires Hive Notebook Pro.

A colony has died, been sold or been united: what should I do?

Open the hive, then ⋯ › Retire the Hive, and choose the reason. The hive leaves the list of active hives but keeps its full history, and its removal is recorded in the register. “Delete”, on the other hand, erases the hive and the register lines that concerned only that hive: prefer “Retire”.

Does the app make the annual hive declaration for me?

No. In France, the app reminds you of it in the To Do tab from 1 September to 31 December, and “Declare Online” opens the official page on service-public.gouv.fr. Once your declaration is made, tap “Done”: the reminder will come back the following year. Outside France, this reminder is not shown.

My end-of-treatment reminder does not arrive. Why?

Check that notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings › Notifications › My Hives. In the app's Settings, “End-of-treatment reminder” must be turned on. The reminder is sent at 9 a.m. on the planned end date, only if the treatment has a planned end date and has not already ended. Also check that no Focus mode is active at that time.

What does Hive Notebook Pro include and how much does it cost?

The free version has no time limit: one apiary, as many hives as you want, inspections, treatments, harvests, the register on screen and backups. Hive Notebook Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription, €9.99 in France (the price varies by country): unlimited apiaries and export of the register as a PDF. It supports Family Sharing. On a new device, open Settings › Discover Pro › “Restore Purchases”.

Where is my notebook stored, and how do I back it up?

Only on your device: there is no account, no server and no syncing between devices. In Settings › “Save a Backup”, the app creates a file that you can keep in Files or iCloud Drive. To change iPhone or move to an iPad, send this file to the other device and choose “Restore a Backup”: the notebook on that device is then replaced by the one in the file.
