Privacy Policy

Hive Notebook · Last updated: 2026-11-24

Other languages ↓

In short

Hive Notebook collects no data. Everything you record in the app stays on your iPhone or iPad.

  • No account, no advertising, no tracking: the app contains no analytics or advertising tools and does not identify you in any way.

Your notebook and your register

The details of your holding (name, address, beekeeper number, business number, phone, veterinarian), your apiaries, your hives, your inspections, your treatments, your harvests and the movements of your colonies are stored in the app's private storage, on your device. They are not sent to anyone, not even to the developer.

Deleting the app erases the whole notebook. It may be included in your device's iCloud or local backups, which you manage yourself in Apple's settings.

Exports and backups

The PDF of the register and the backup file are created only when you ask for them, and you choose where to save them or who to send them to. They contain the details of your holding: the app has no control over a file once you have shared it.

Notifications

End-of-treatment reminders are scheduled on the device; no server is involved.

Link to the hive declaration

The “Declare Online” button, shown in France, opens a page of the service-public.gouv.fr website in your browser. The app sends it no information; that site applies its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Hive Notebook Pro is sold and managed by Apple. The app sees neither your payment method nor your identity; it only reads, through the App Store, whether the purchase has been made, in order to unlock the features.

Children

Hive Notebook collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
