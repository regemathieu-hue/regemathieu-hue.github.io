Privacy Policy

Pattoune · Last updated: 2026-11-21

Other languages ↓

In short

Pattoune collects no personal data. The app has no server and requires no account: your data is stored on your devices and in your own iCloud, and the developer has no access to it.

Your data

The app stores what you enter for your pets: profile (name, photo, microchip, vet, insurance…), care and vaccines, treatments and doses, weight, food, stocks, the health journal and its photos, walks, expenses and documents.

This data is synced between your devices through iCloud (your account's private database, run by Apple). It never goes through any server of the developer.

Sharing with your household

If you share a pet, the people you invite see and edit everything about it, including walk routes. Sharing goes through iCloud; you can stop it at any time from the pet's page, and each guest can leave it.

Location

Your location is used only during a walk that you start, including with the screen locked, and then GPS stops. The route is saved with the walk in your iCloud; it is never sent to the developer.

  • In the app's Settings, “Keep walk routes” lets you keep only the duration and distance.
  • You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Photos, camera and notifications

  • Photos: only the photos you choose are imported (pet photo, journal, documents).
  • Camera: it is used to scan your documents, when you ask for it.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your device; no outside notification service is used.

Siri, widgets and exports

Requests to Siri are handled by Apple under its own rules. Widgets read a summary stored on your device. Exports (text, PDF, “lost pet” poster, pet-sitter sheet) leave the app only if you share them, through iOS sharing tools; they are then subject to the terms of the service you choose.

Purchases, ads and tracking

The Pattoune Premium subscription is sold and managed by Apple; the developer receives no payment data. The app contains no ads, no trackers and no analytics or usage statistics tools.

Children

The app collects no data and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
