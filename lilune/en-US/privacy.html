Privacy Policy

Lilune · Last updated: 2026-11-21

Other languages ↓

In short

Lilune collects no personal data. The app has no server and asks for no account: your data is stored on your devices and in your own iCloud, and the developer has no access to it.

Your data

The app stores what you enter for your baby: first name, date of birth, sex if you enter it, photo, feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, health (temperatures, medicine), growth measurements, appointments and notes.

This data is synced between your devices through iCloud (your account's private database, managed by Apple). It never goes through a server of the developer.

Sharing with the other parent

If you share a baby, the people you invite see and edit everything about them. Sharing goes through iCloud; you can stop it at any time, and each invitee can leave it.

Photos and notifications

  • Photos: only the photo you choose for your baby is imported.
  • Notifications: appointment and feeding reminders are scheduled on your device; no outside notification service is used.

Widgets, timers and exports

Widgets read a summary stored on your device, and the timers shown on the Lock Screen stay on your device. The PDF export only leaves the app if you share it, using iOS tools; it is then subject to the terms of the service you choose.

Purchases, ads and tracking

Lilune Pro is sold by Apple; the developer receives no payment data. The app contains no ads, no trackers and no analytics or usage statistics tools.

Children

The app is meant for parents. It collects no data, and the information about your child stays in your iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
