FAQ

How do I share the log with the other parent?

Open the "Baby" tab, then "Share with the other parent", and send the invitation by Messages, Mail or WhatsApp. The other parent taps the link on their iPhone: they see and complete the same log with their own iCloud account. Both devices need to be signed in to iCloud. Sharing is part of Lilune Pro, but the other parent doesn't have to buy anything.

What does Lilune Pro include?

The free version lets you log everything for one baby with no time limit: bottles, nursing, sleep, diapers, pumping, health, growth, appointments, widgets and night-light mode. Lilune Pro, a one-time purchase with no subscription, adds sharing with the other parent, next nap prediction, statistics, PDF export, multiple babies and a choice of app icons. It is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

How do I get my purchase back on a new device?

Open the Lilune Pro window and tap "Restore Purchases", with the same App Store account you used to buy it.

How does the nap prediction work?

Lilune starts from the usual awake time for your baby's age, then adjusts it with their actual awake periods over the last few days. The result is an estimated time with a range, never a rule: every baby has their own rhythm.

Does Lilune replace the pediatrician?

No. Lilune is a logbook: it keeps track of feeds, sleep, diapers and growth, and reminds you of appointments. It gives no medical advice. If you have any doubt about your baby's health, ask a healthcare professional.

Do the timers keep running when I close the app?

Yes. A nursing session or sleep you start keeps counting, shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, and also appears for the other parent if you share the log.

How do I switch iPhones without losing my data?

Your data is in your iCloud: sign in with the same iCloud account on the new iPhone and install Lilune, and your log comes back on its own.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a baby from their profile ("Edit profile"): everything about them is erased. Deleting the app is not enough, because the data stays in your iCloud: to erase everything, open Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage, then Lilune.

Privacy Policy

Lilune collects no personal data and contains no ads or trackers. Your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud; the developer has no access to it.
