FAQ

Which cameras can I use?

The iPad or iPhone camera, a Wi-Fi camera (Lumix, Canon), a GoPro (Wi-Fi and Bluetooth) or an HDMI capture card.

My Wi-Fi camera isn't detected, what should I do?

Check that the iPad is connected to the camera's Wi-Fi and that Flashbooth has the “Local Network” permission in Settings › Flashbooth.

How do guests get their photos?

Via AirDrop, Messages, Mail or by saving to Photos. If you enable the online gallery, a QR code gives access to the event page.

How does the online gallery work?

It is an option that is off by default. It sends the event's photos to a server whose address and access key you enter yourself. You can delete the photos stored on that server at any time from the app.

How do I stop guests from leaving the app?

Use Flashbooth's kiosk mode, and turn on iOS Guided Access (Settings › Accessibility › Guided Access) to lock the iPad to the app.
