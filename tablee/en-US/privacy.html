Privacy Policy

Tablée · Last updated: 2026-11-02

Other languages ↓

In short

Tablée doesn't collect any personal data. The app works offline, doesn't connect to any server and requires no account. Everything you enter stays on your device, unless you choose to share or export it.

Your data

The app saves your games, their scores and rules, your list of players (name, emoji, color), the statistics calculated from these games and your settings. A player's name can be anything you like: it's only useful to you.

This data is saved on your device, and the developer has no access to it. Like other apps' data, it may be included in your device backups if you've turned them on. You can erase everything in the settings; deleting the app erases it too.

Sharing, export and import

Nothing leaves the app without action on your part.

  • Sharing a game: the image or text goes through iOS tools (AirDrop, Messages, Mail…) and contains the players' names and scores.
  • Export: the JSON or CSV file contains your games and the players' names. You choose where to save it; it's then subject to the terms of the service you chose.
  • Import: the app only reads the file you select.

Permissions requested

Tablée doesn't ask for any permission: no camera, location, contacts or photos.

Ads, purchases and tracking

Tablée is free. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no trackers and no analytics or usage statistics tools.

Children

The app doesn't collect any data and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
