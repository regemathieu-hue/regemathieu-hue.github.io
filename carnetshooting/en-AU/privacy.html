Privacy Policy

Shootbook · Last updated: 2026-10-24

Other languages ↓

In short

Shootbook collects no personal data and has no server. Everything you enter stays on your device, and in your own iCloud account if you turn on sync.

Your shoots

Text, images, locations, lists and team contacts are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also stored in your iCloud account, managed by Apple, so you can find them on your other devices. The developer has no access to them.

Planning together and sending

When you share a shoot live, its content is stored in your iCloud and made available only to the people you invite, through Apple's sharing service. You can stop sharing at any time. When you send a shoot or a call sheet, it only goes to the people you choose, by the means you choose (AirDrop, Messages, Mail…).

Permissions requested

Each permission is only requested when you use the feature concerned, and you can withdraw it in your device's Settings.

  • Photos: only the images you pick are copied into the app, at a reduced size. An image is only saved to your library when you ask.
  • Camera: to photograph a location while scouting, an outfit or a reference pose.
  • Location: only when you tap “I'm here now” or “My location”, to pin a place on the map. It stays on your device.
  • Notifications: the reminder the day before, scheduled on your device.

Maps and web pages

To show the address of a point or search for a place, the coordinates or the text you type are sent to Apple's map service. When you share a web page to the app (a Pinterest link, for example), the extension downloads that page directly from your device to get its main image. As with any internet connection, these services see the IP address of the request.

Widget

The widget shows the title, time, location and cover of your next shoot, using the data stored on your device.

Advertising and tracking

Shootbook contains no advertising, no trackers and no analytics or usage statistics tools.

Children

The app collects no data and is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: rege.mathieu@icloud.com
